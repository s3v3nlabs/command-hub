# Bounty / Licensed Disclosure

Legal business channel for crowdsourced / bounty / licensed disclosure of worthy findings.

**Status workflow:** `draft` → `review` → `submitted`  
**Hold:** `HOLD_VYRNA_UNSYNC` · `lattice_sync: false`

## What this is

- UI: `/bounty`
- API: `GET/POST /api/bounty`
- Authorized disclosure drafts only

## What this is NOT

- Exploit binary / PoC / crash-trigger payload generation
- Unauthorized access tooling
- A claim of Vyrna lattice sync

Weaponized content is rejected by the store.

## Policy

Prefer coordinated / licensed disclosure with the asset owner. Keep Privacy Fortress: no prompt-body telemetry; no secrets in summaries.
